## Case reader

[../notes/midterm-case-reader.html](../notes/midterm-case-reader.html) shows the real pages of both past papers, both answer keys, the 2024 figures and four readings the papers lean on, with highlights painted on the words. Red highlights mean "watch out": a trap that costs marks, or a place where the key, the question or two sources disagree. Amber highlights mean "what it means": the economics behind the passage. Each highlight opens a note with a small diagram and a link back to the matching section of the study page. The "Watch out only" filter is a fast pass for the night before. The study page links into it from its "Case reader" section and from a "Read it highlighted" line under every question.

I HIGHLY recommend that you attempt the versions with no answers in their entirety before reviewing the answers. I have also published a different practice exam on askmyprof.com. This version allows you to take the exam and receive an AI generated grade."

The askmyprof.com practice exam is not in this folder. Take it online after you have tried both past papers.
