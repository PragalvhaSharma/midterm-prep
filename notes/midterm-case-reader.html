Turn 70% of jobs and 25% of GDP into output per worker

A sector's share of GDP divided by its share of jobs gives its output per worker relative to the national average.

Mozambique farms 25 ÷ 70 ≈ 0.36 Mozambique non-farm 75 ÷ 30 = 2.5 Gap 2.5 ÷ 0.36 ≈ 7× Germany farms 1 ÷ 1 = 1.0

Room to catch up and the case for structural transformation both sit in this one fact.

The idea here is that the focus must shift from investment per se to infusion of new ideas available abroad, and then on to domestic innovation.
