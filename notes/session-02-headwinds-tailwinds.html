The four attacks coming, and your answer

Attack 1

“Negotiations broke down.”

You: Symmetric, bounded, calibrated. $27.6bn each way, dollar for dollar, support announced alongside. Both governments chose proportionality. CUSMA’s review is multi year; one bad round is not the end, and the rules of origin shielding 85% were untouched.
Attack 2

“109,000 jobs lost, youth at 13.8%.”

You: January and February figures, the worst point of the year. It reversed: 6.4% by July with private hiring up. And with the population falling, a stable rate means employment holding against a shrinking labour force. Concede the youth number, then call it structural, not cyclical.
Attack 3

“Population fell 55,025.”

You: A policy choice, not a shock, reset annually. Canada grew GDP while GDP per capita stagnated from 2022 to 2024. This reverses exactly that. If the goal is living standards, it is the policy working. Their best card. Half answer it and move.
Attack 4

“The AI premium is 5.1% and not significant.”

You: Backward looking, at 2024–25 adopters. Its finding is about sequencing, not a ceiling. Adoption went 12.2% to 19.2% in a year. And quote the range against zero, not against America.
Concede early: “None of this fixes productivity, and with population growth at zero that is the only engine left. I am optimistic about twelve months and worried about ten years. Different claims.”
Close: “Their case needs three things to go wrong at once and needs eighteen months of adaptation to suddenly stop. Real 30% risk. Not a base case.”
Never say tariffs do not matter Never wave away 13.8% youth unemployment Concede once, early, and mean it
